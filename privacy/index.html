Privacy

November 13, 2026

Cookt does not ask you to make an account.

On your iPhone

Cookt keeps your cooking on the phone: the nights you cooked, your streak, how many nights you meant to cook, grocery checks, servings, which days are on the shopping list, the reminder switch, and a Cookt Mode session you have not finished. That stays in storage on the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

This week’s dinners

When the phone is online, Cookt asks our server for the published week. The request is the date range. It does not include your name. The week is saved on the phone, so dinner still opens with no connection.

Reminders

If you turn the reminder on, the iPhone schedules a local notification at 5pm. A running step timer can also schedule a local notification for when that step is done. Cookt does not send remote push notifications.

Product events

Cookt sends anonymous events to PostHog about what happened in the app: onboarding, the trial, a recipe, Cookt Mode, a cook, a share, the paywall, a purchase, and a streak freeze. The events do not include your name or email. Cookt does not record the screen.

Cookt+

Apple charges you for weekly, yearly, or lifetime. RevenueCat tells the app whether Cookt+ is active, using an anonymous id, so the week can unlock. Cookt does not receive your card number.

Sharing

The share sheet is Apple’s. Cookt makes the sticker on your phone and does not see where you send it.
