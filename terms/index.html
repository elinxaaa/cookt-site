Cookt gives you one dinner at a time, the steps to cook it, and a record of the nights you cooked. That record stays on your iPhone. Cookt does not ask you to make an account.

Cookt+

Tonight is free. The rest of the week, the shopping list, and the previous four weeks are Cookt+. The first seven days of Cookt+ are free. After that, Apple charges the price you confirm.

Weekly and yearly renew until you cancel. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Canceling stops the next charge. You keep Cookt+ until the end of the period you already paid for.

Lifetime is one charge. It keeps Cookt+ unlocked while Cookt is offered. If Cookt stops offering the app, the unlock ends with it.

Apple bills every Cookt+ purchase and decides refunds. Restore a purchase from the You tab in Cookt.
